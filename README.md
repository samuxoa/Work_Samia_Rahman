## Projects

###  AquaSense: Smart Irrigation & Greenhouse Automation
A low-cost, Arduino Uno–based greenhouse system. Soil moisture and humidity sensors classify the soil as *Dry*, *Moist* or *Over-watered* and send the status to a phone over Bluetooth, while a fan regulates the greenhouse climate.

`Arduino Uno` `IoT` `Bluetooth` `Soil moisture sensor`

###  Farmer's Assistant App (WICE 2025, Team BANGLAR MUKH)
A mobile app that gives farmers live sensor data, crop-specific recommendations for temperature, humidity and water levels, and an AI chatbot for farming questions.
